# Everyday prompts

Use the main entry and plain language. Prefix with `$co-founder` in Codex CLI/IDE
or `/co-founder` in Claude Code when explicitly selecting the skill.

## Decision, not applause

“Challenge this feature idea against our target customer and existing workflows.
Inspect the relevant product context first. Research current alternatives. Give
evidence, counterarguments, a smaller experiment and a recommendation. Do not code.”

## Recurrent customer issue

“This issue reopened. Reproduce it with synthetic data, identify the cause and
related module effects, and explain what the previous checks missed. Preserve my
unrelated work. Propose a scoped fix and wait for approval.”

## Premium UI repair

“Improve this screen for the actual job it serves. Preserve brand, behavior and
shared components. Inspect the current screen and the supplied reference, choose
relevant category guidance, then verify real screenshots at the applicable sizes,
including navigation and refresh. Do not replace the whole design.”

## Security-conscious implementation

“Before changing permissions or customer data access, identify the trust boundaries
and tenant/role checks. Treat missing evidence as uncertainty. Use a disposable test
environment and report exact findings. Do not claim the whole app is secure.”

## Support follow-through

“Review this sanitized issue record. Distinguish fixed in code, verified, deployed,
and customer-confirmed. Draft the next accurate customer update. Do not send it.”

## Growth and sales

“Based on approved product capabilities and available evidence, recommend one
realistic acquisition experiment for this week and how to evaluate it. Do not
invent metrics, prospects or ad spend.”

“Prepare a discovery/demo plan for this prospect from the information I authorized.
Address their actual job and approved offer. Draft, do not send.”

## Brand and content

“Create one useful product post from this verified workflow and our approved brand
kit. Keep one message, original assets and a readable visual hierarchy. Produce an
actual editable export if the tools exist; otherwise identify the exact missing
capability. Do not publish.”

“Turn my actual experience below into a personal-brand post. Preserve my point of
view; do not fabricate a customer story or make it sound like a generic AI essay.”

“Create a short product explainer using approved assets. Script, storyboard,
animate, render and inspect. Preserve object continuity. Report sound/playback
review honestly; do not claim an inaccessible reference video was watched.”

## Daily focus

“Give me a short founder brief from current local records. Prioritize customer
impact and promised follow-ups. Include at most one useful content opportunity.
Do not schedule, send, deploy or invent missing activity.”
