# Research record and limitations

Checked 2026-09-23. Exact source URLs and access statuses are in
research-catalog.json (`../skills/co-founder/references/research-catalog.json`, historical reference).

## What was reviewed

Official documentation for Codex, Claude Code, Agent Skills, accessibility,
application security and optional browser/video tools. Public source-page text
for a representative range of product/website categories: Linear, Stripe, Apple,
Aesop, Vercel, Aman, charity: water, One Medical, Brilliant, Airbnb, Pentagram,
sweetgreen, Work & Co, Shopify, Grainger and The New Yorker.

Motion research includes source case pages from Ordinary Folk and BUCK, a Giant Ant
work index, and Figma’s practitioner interview. Brand/campaign research includes
Figma’s Config identity account, Spotify’s Wrapped campaign explanation, and
editorial discovery sources. Two accessible stills were visually inspected: a
BUCK motion thumbnail and a Spotify campaign club illustration.

## What was not reviewed

External live-site browser captures failed because of environment network/DNS
restrictions and a browser navigation policy (ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ADMINISTRATOR). Some CDN imagery was unavailable. Requested YouTube
material and embedded external films were not played in full. LinkedIn brand feeds
and Apple's Instagram feed were blocked. No feed-wide audit, measured engagement
comparison, full external-film viewing, audio analysis or exhaustive review of
“all categories” is claimed.

The 17 category guides are representative original recommendations, not reports
of pixel-perfect audits of those websites. Motion durations are starting heuristics,
not timing measured from inaccessible footage. The included generated samples are
our own original artifacts, separate from the research references.

## How to finish a reference review later

In a browser-enabled host, choose a small number of references relevant to the
actual category and audience. Capture desktop/mobile states, play films completely,
record meaningful timestamps and inspect authorized posts. Use
reference-review.md (`../skills/co-founder/assets/templates/reference-review.md`, historical reference).
Keep source observation separate from proposed application. Never claim an image
or film was seen based only on its title, alt text, metadata or a search result.

Do not redistribute source artwork, videos, logos, fonts or music without rights.
Learn hierarchy, narrative and coherent systems; do not clone recognizable identity.
