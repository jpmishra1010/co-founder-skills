Two accessible **Linear UI stills** were directly inspected: one application view
and one multi-view comparison. The observations concern navigation/context

claims about untested live interactions or an exhaustive mobile audit.

was reviewed. Earlier source-page research across representative website categories

## Access limits remain explicit

External browser navigation was blocked in the build environment. The accessible
images were inspected through returned image content; this did not make live-site
navigation available. Some Figma/Ordinary Folk CDN requests failed. No restricted
social feed, engagement dataset, full playlist or external film was fully reviewed.
There is **no** assertion that a video's title or thumbnail equals watching it.

The original v1 research included two other accessible stills and public page text.
See the historical report in `docs/history/`. New work should choose relevant,
accessible references in the user's environment and record actual viewing.

## What was inspected in our own work

The new exported desktop/mobile UI states and campaign PNG were visually inspected.
The mobile detail problem found during review was corrected and regression-tested.
Seven authored motion styleframes were inspected; all 720 exported frames received
marked-element geometry checks and four semantic state checkpoints were exercised.
Neither a geometry check nor a sampled contact sheet proves full real-time viewing.
The included video is silent, and full human audiovisual/owner acceptance remains
outstanding. This is recorded rather than converted into a false completion claim.

## How future skills must research

Use the actual approved project assets first. Choose references relevant to the
surface, audience and task. Inspect images/live states; for motion, record genuine
viewing timestamps and analyze the complete relevant sequence when tools permit.
Separate observation from recommendation, label blocked access and never copy a
recognizable brand identity or redistribute reference media/font files without rights.
