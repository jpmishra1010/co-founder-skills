# Creative work: identity → idea → craft → review

## Four distinct disciplines

**Product UI:** task hierarchy, useful density, actual states, accessibility,
responsive interaction and existing components. A grid of cards is not the goal.

**Marketing website:** audience, offer, proof, editorial rhythm, imagery and a
clear next action. Do not import dashboard density into a landing page or force
a luxury/art direction onto an industrial catalogue.

**Post/campaign:** one defensible message and a visual idea that adds meaning.
Inspect at real feed size. A logo, large headline and empty boxes do not constitute
a campaign concept. Platform dimensions must be checked against current needs.

**Motion:** make an action/result or metaphor understandable. Write a story,
approve strong still frames, then animate causal transitions and continuity.
Do not give all scenes identical durations or replace storytelling with fades.

## Shared gates

1. **Bind:** exact project, audience, brand, surface, assets, permissions and facts.
2. **Inspect:** actual relevant visual references, using current owned references
   first. Record what was seen and how. Page text is not a viewed design. A video
   thumbnail is not a viewed film. Blocked access requires an accessible alternative
   or a clearly exploratory outcome—not a fabricated research claim.
3. **Choose a concept:** for an unfamiliar brief show a small number of genuinely
   distinct directions. Get approval before expensive production. For an already
   accepted campaign reuse its direction without asking the same question again.
4. **Produce:** use the project's actual component/design system, owned asset
   compositing or editable scene tool. No universal renderer is mandatory. Build
   reusable formats only after a direction works. Preserve source assets.
5. **Inspect and revise:** record a specific weakness at a named location/time,
   change it, and re-inspect. Check live interaction, viewport, feed size, relevant
   motion transitions and complete playback/audio as applicable. Do not repeatedly
   rerender everything when a scoped revision is enough.
6. **Handoff:** editable source, actual exports, asset register, checks, limitations
   and acceptance status. Rendered, technically checked, visually reviewed and
   owner accepted are distinct labels.

## What the review gate does

`quality_gate.py` checks the structure of a record against the selected workflow.
It requires specific evidence references, visual observations and revisions for
creative work. It refuses to treat frame samples as full video playback. It never
returns "publish approved" and cannot verify whether a reported inspection actually
happened. Approval and tool permissions must be enforced outside the document.

For routine edits to an already accepted direction, cite the approved project
reference and describe the targeted change; do not force a new brand-research
project or three mockups every time. For a genuinely new direction, explicit
acceptance is worth the extra step. Reuse context until its fingerprint changes.

## Connected example lessons

The new Northline fixtures connect one record through repair, stock and reporting.
UI uses a practical list/detail workflow; the post uses a tangible service ticket;
the video reuses one record through transformation and a repeat-request check.
The palette and owned logo stay consistent, but the composition differs by medium.

The example UI was revised after visual inspection exposed an offscreen mobile
detail problem: selecting a row now opens a focused detail state with a back
control and focus restoration. Browser tests verify that behavior. That is a
specific craft/flow correction—not a claim of universally perfect UI.

Examples are fictional. Reference images and fonts are not redistributed. External
films were not watched in full in the build environment. The included movie is
silent; its temporal state/geometry were tested and keyframes inspected, but full
human audiovisual acceptance remains open. Review it before public promotion.
