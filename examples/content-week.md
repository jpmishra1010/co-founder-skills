# Synthetic, evidence-led content week

This is an example planning structure, not a claim about a real product or schedule.

Day 1: explain a verified workflow users repeatedly ask about. Use actual approved
assets, one clear message and an accurate next action. Draft, do not publish.

Day 2: review a prospect's real objection and prepare a tailored demonstration;
there may be no need for another public post.

Day 3: turn the founder's actual lesson into a personal post after confirming the
point of view. Do not invent a story about customers or revenue.

Day 4: create a short approved product demonstration. Reuse the brand system but
make motion explain the workflow. Inspect the render and sound separately.

Day 5: review outcomes and unresolved customer issues. Measure qualified actions
from available data, not just output volume. Change one thing next week.

A daily brief runs on demand unless a supported host scheduler is explicitly
configured with a timezone, permissions and a draft-only policy.
