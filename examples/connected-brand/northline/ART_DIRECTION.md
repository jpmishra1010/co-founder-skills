# Northline: fictional connected-brand fixture

This is an original repair-operations brand used only to test the skill pack.
It is not MyBizMaster, JyotishGrid, or an existing customer company. The approval
fields in the fixture mean “approved by the fixture author,” not accepted by the
user as premium. Never inherit this palette or sample product facts elsewhere.

**Idea:** one repair record, not a collection of disconnected admin tasks.
**Visual grammar:** structural N mark; calm forest ink; warm neutral campaign
paper; precise application typography; thin dividers rather than nested boxes.
UI is deliberately denser than marketing. Posts use the service slip as the
hero, not a flowchart. Motion carries the same record through one transaction.

**User task:** inspect a repair, move it to ready, and see one stock deduction.
**Preserved invariant:** retrying the action must not deduct stock again.
**Synthetic evidence:** all names, repair IDs and quantities are fabricated test
data. This fixture has in-memory state, not authentication or a backend.

**Sources inspected:** Linear's published after-interface screenshot and its
multi-view exploration image informed hierarchy and stress-testing, not copying.
BUCK's IBM Think still informed material depth as a controlled emphasis, not its
palette or letterform identity. These are research observations, not our assets.

**Review policy:** unit/render checks are separate from visual inspection and
owner acceptance. Motion playback and audio review must be reported honestly.
