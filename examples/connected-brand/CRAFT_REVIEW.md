# Authoring review — not owner acceptance

## Reference access

Accessible Linear UI stills were visually inspected for task hierarchy, practical
density and consistency between contexts. A BUCK/IBM still was inspected for
controlled surface/depth treatment. Source page texts were also reviewed. No source
film was watched in full, and a brand social feed was not exhaustively audited.
No reference imagery was copied into these samples. See the research log.

## Observations and revisions

**UI:** the previous v1 fixture devoted too much space to generic metric cards and
demo explanations. The replacement centers on a realistic queue/detail task with
8 synthetic records, discrete status cues, clear next action and restrained chrome.
Desktop and mobile screenshots were actually inspected. A further mobile issue was
found: the selected detail originally appeared below the full queue. It was changed
to a focused detail state with back navigation/focus restoration, and browser tests
exercise both. This is a source-level correction, not simply a nicer screenshot.

**Post:** the earlier three-box diagram was replaced by a tangible service ticket
and its single stock consequence. The large sans/serif headline establishes the
message while a quieter secondary slip resolves reporting. The full exported PNG was inspected:
headline, slanted-ticket bounds, stock transition, overlapping slip and footer were
legible at source size. The main claim is intentionally large; small illustrative
record labels are supporting detail, not essential text to cram into a feed preview.

**Motion:** equal-duration, repeated card scenes were replaced with one object
transforming through a story. The completion event, stock count, retry guard and
report use the same R-1048 record and shared palette. Seven main styleframes were
inspected, plus render checks over all 720 frames and four declared-state checkpoints.
These do not prove a full human real-time viewing or sound review. The movie remains
silent. Owner acceptance and full playback are explicitly outstanding.

**Brand continuity:** one exact dark/light logo pair and selected type/color rules
are bound from Northline's own manifest; a separate Fieldnote proof verifies that
the same neutral template does not inherit Northline's identity.
