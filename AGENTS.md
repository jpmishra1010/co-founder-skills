telemetry, external upload, hidden account access, or unattended paid calls.
Use synthetic data only. Never commit `.co-founder/`, customer records, real brand
assets, downloaded reference media, font binaries, access tokens, or local logs.

Keep the entry skill small. Put conditional guidance in workflow/reference files.

visual or playback review from code alone. Preserve installation/uninstallation
safety and test adversarial paths. Every factual external claim needs a source.

local tests from live Codex/Claude evaluation. Do not inflate research coverage.
