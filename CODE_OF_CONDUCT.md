# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique implementations and claims,
not people. No harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, or deceptive
promotion. Do not post private customer information, credentials, or security
exploit details in public threads. Maintainers may edit/remove violating content
and restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository's available maintainer contact; request one without disclosing private
facts publicly. Apply the same expectations in issues, reviews, and discussions.
