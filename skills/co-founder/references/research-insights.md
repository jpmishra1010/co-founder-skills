# Transfer principles, not another brand’s identity

Read `research-catalog.json` for source dates, exact URLs, and access limitations.
These are original applications of the reviewed source material, not measurements
of inaccessible websites or claims of watching films.

## A system can be expressive without being arbitrary
Figma’s Config 2026 account describes a controlled visual vocabulary carried
across media and a two-way dialogue between static design and motion. Co-founder
should therefore define a small brand grammar and vary composition deliberately.
Do not copy its glyphs or use “human” as an excuse for random noise. [SOCIAL-01]

## Content needs a real underlying story
Spotify’s Wrapped campaign account ties a visual identity to personal listening
stories. For a business product, use actual approved workflow evidence or genuine
founder experience rather than inventing statistics for a lookalike recap.
The inspected club artwork demonstrates a clear silhouette and coherent material
finish; it does not prove campaign performance. [SOCIAL-02, SOCIAL-04]

## Motion changes how information arrives
The Figma practitioner interview discusses timing, transitions, holds and sound
as ways to direct attention. In this pack, each scene should advance one idea and
reserve time to read the result. Reference real mechanisms and editorial rhythm,
not only whatever movement is fashionable. Our durations are suggested starting
points, not observations from a watched film. [MOTION-07]

## Material choice should support meaning
BUCK’s financial-brand case connects materials and geometric relationships to
its communication task. The inspected thumbnail supports limited observations
about surface contrast and organized perspective; it does not reveal pacing.
For a dense business UI, clear status and typography may be more appropriate
than a decorative 3D scene. [MOTION-04, MOTION-05]

## Research to finish in a browser-enabled session
Inspect real desktop/mobile screenshots of selected category references. Watch
selected films fully, then log shot ranges, transitions, holds, audio and limits.
Inspect actual authorized social posts, recording date and content rather than
assuming popularity. Use `assets/templates/reference-review.md`. Never backfill
“watched” statuses from titles, thumbnails or page descriptions.
