# Brand-led social and personal content

## Message before decoration
Choose one audience, one relevant problem or observation, one defensible message,
and one next action. Separate graphic headline from caption. A graphic need not
contain every feature, caveat, hashtag, and contact detail. Use evidence to decide
what belongs in the claim; do not invent numbers to make a layout more impressive.

## Content formats
Demonstration: show the actual job and resulting state.
Practical lesson: explain one useful decision or technique.
Verified release: who benefits, what changed, and how to use it.
Problem/solution: show the mechanism, not only a vague promise.
Founder reflection: a real observation, decision, tradeoff, and lesson.
Customer evidence: consented and accurately represented, with necessary context.
Process: honest behind-the-scenes work using non-sensitive material.
Comparison: fair criteria and current supported facts, not unverified attacks.
Carousel: a coherent argument with one beat per slide and consistent continuity.

## Art direction
Choose a format family: typography-led editorial, product proof, simple diagram,
photography-led story, or campaign motif. Use approved brand assets and repeat a
small vocabulary. Give the main idea a clear focal point; use supporting material
only when it explains something. Preserve exact logos rather than regenerating
them. Leave a designated brand area blank when the correct asset is unavailable.
Avoid fake device UI, decorative analytics, stock handshakes, irrelevant 3D objects,
arbitrary neon/glass/orbs, and gradients used as a substitute for hierarchy.

These are quality heuristics, not universal bans: an expressive brand may use
color, texture, or unusual type purposefully. Match the user's accepted references.
Do not turn every brand into a copy of Apple, Notion, or a generic SaaS launch.

## Copy
Use concrete nouns, ordinary verbs, and the vocabulary of the customer. Show the
workflow/mechanism. Remove unsupported superlatives, manufactured urgency, repetitive
slogans, excessive punctuation, and generic "unlock/revolutionize" phrasing when it
adds no meaning. Personal-brand posts require the founder's actual viewpoint;
ask for the missing experience instead of fabricating an emotional origin story.
Preserve requested language and script. Verify multilingual text with a competent
reviewer when necessary; automatic transliteration is not native-style writing.

## Production contract
Record channel and intended dimensions. Treat bundled 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, and 16:9
presets as composition presets, not promises of current platform specifications.
Check official placement requirements before paid campaigns or platform-specific
exports. Recompose for each aspect ratio. Test the feed-size thumbnail and final
render. Include alt text, caption, editable source, and asset provenance.

## Reference use
Read `research-catalog.json` for the access status of brand research. Official
campaign/editorial posts can inform message hierarchy; blocked social feeds cannot
be claimed as reviewed. Never infer engagement or conversion effectiveness from
an attractive design. Do not redistribute brand media or imitate a distinctive
campaign too closely. Publish only after artifact/account/text approval.
