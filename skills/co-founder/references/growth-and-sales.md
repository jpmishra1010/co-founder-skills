# Connected growth and sales

Start from a real bottleneck and available data. Separate acquisition, activation,
retention, referral, and sales follow-through. Do not prescribe daily posting on
five channels to a solo founder without capacity or evidence.

A useful positioning statement specifies the customer, job, current alternative,
meaningful difference, and credible proof. An unbuilt feature or synthetic sample
is not proof. Keep a claims ledger with source, status, date, and permission.

A bounded growth experiment records hypothesis, audience, channel, message,
artifact, baseline, observation window, primary measure, cost/time cap, and stop
criterion. Use current official platform guidance for placement, tracking, consent,
and advertising changes. Do not claim statistically meaningful improvements from
a tiny or confounded sample. Include manual reporting when integrations are absent.

For SEO and AI discovery, inspect crawlability, accurate page meaning, technical
indexing, canonical content, useful documentation, and credible public evidence.
Do not guarantee rank, citations by an AI assistant, or a traffic target.

Sales work is prospect-specific. Use discovery to uncover fit, not manufacture
agreement. Tailor a demonstration to a real workflow using authorized/synthetic
data. Record objections honestly. Distinguish released, preview, and proposed
capabilities. Proposals require approved pricing, scope, exclusions, responsibilities,
and dates. Do not invent discounts, contracts, or delivery promises.

Prepare a next action and follow-up draft. Sending needs correct recipient, current
thread, explicit approval, consent/platform compliance where relevant, and an
actual tool result. Respect opt-outs; do not use sensitive traits to manipulate
people. Promote evidence from support and sales only after sanitizing it and
obtaining appropriate permission for quotations or customer identification.
