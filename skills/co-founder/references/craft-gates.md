# Craft gates: separate operation from quality

No amount of passing geometry tests establishes premium design. Record technical,
creative and human acceptance separately. One self-review is not an independent
reviewer. Use `quality_gate.py` to validate the review record, not to grade taste.

## Gate 0 — Identity and brief
Resolve the project bundle and chosen surface. Name the user/audience, job, one
message, required real assets, exact copy/claims, dimensions and preserve/change
boundary. Missing identity or materially conflicting brand sources stops release.

## Gate 1 — Observe, do not name-drop
For a new direction inspect 2–3 relevant accessible references and actual project
screens. Record observation, evidence path/source, what transfers, and what must
not be copied. Existing accepted project work may be enough for a scoped repair.
Text-only access is not image inspection. A thumbnail is not a watched film.
When tools block access, choose an accessible substitute or label exploration;
do not certify reference-informed output from search summaries.

## Gate 2 — Resolve the concept
UI: a content-realistic representative screen and important states.
Website: page narrative, section rhythm and a hero tied to the business.
Post: one visual idea that adds information beyond the headline.
Video: storyboard + strong styleframes + object-continuity and sound plans.
Offer 2–3 genuinely different directions only for a broad new brief, not every
routine asset. Reuse accepted art direction until the brief or identity changes.
No automatic “headline, three boxes, footer” layout. No one palette for all brands.

## Gate 3 — Produce with appropriate tools
Do not ask a constrained starter renderer to substitute for bespoke composition.
Use the project's components and design tools; native image generation, vector,
HTML/CSS, approved photo editing, or a configured video tool as appropriate.
Keep exact logos/text in controlled layers. Do not redraw an existing screen as
proof. Working concept assets can be illustrative only when labeled accurately.
Produce an editable source and an actual export when the environment supports it.

## Gate 4 — Inspect the result and revise
Open the exported artifact. Identify at least the strongest defect, its location,
and a concrete change (or cite evidence that no blocker remains after a revision).
UI: task hierarchy, density, focus, content stress, real navigation and responsive
states. Post: focal point, five-second comprehension, phone-size readability,
exact brand, crop, claim. Motion: first/last frames, every hold and transition,
causal action, object permanence, easing/velocity, full playback and sound sync.
Sampling frames cannot certify full playback or audio. Mark those checks not-run.

## Gate 5 — Deliver at the truthful status
Draft → technically checked → craft-reviewed → owner-accepted → published are
separate states. A rendered demo may be delivered with known gaps, never as an
accepted template. A check returning ready-for-human-review is not an approval.
Wrong brand, unreadable key content, unsupported claims, altered protected assets,
or a broken primary action blocks publish-ready handoff even if code tests pass.
Do not force acceptance by filling every status with “pass”.

Use `assets/templates/quality-review.json`. For targeted fixes, required inspection
is scoped to the change plus connected risks; do not reread all guidelines.
