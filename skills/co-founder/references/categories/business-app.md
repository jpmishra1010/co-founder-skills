# Business software and operations

**Design objective:** Task hierarchy, readable dense data, coherent shared components.

## Apply
Prioritize actual work: search, create, status, exception, audit, next action. Keep tables when comparison matters; do not convert every table into cards. Use restrained surfaces, stable navigation, aligned amounts, clear filter state, and useful empty/error states. Validate repeated operations, roles, persistence, and shared UI across routes.

## Do not confuse polish with usability
Do not import the oversized whitespace of a marketing hero into a working inventory screen.

## Establish a project-specific direction
Record the audience/task, approved brand assets, existing typography/palette,
content density, main action, proof available, and two or three accepted references.
Adapt structure and imagery to the brand rather than cloning a reference. Do not
load other category guides unless the task truly spans them.

## Acceptance
Render actual content and core states at narrow and wide widths. Check readable
text, image crops, hierarchy, interaction, keyboard/focus, refresh/navigation,
long/empty/error data, and primary action. Preserve already-working behavior.
Record what was not tested. Human approval determines taste and brand fit.

## Reference starting points and limits
Linear; Shopify product ecosystem. Consult `../research-catalog.json` for URLs, reviewed dates, and access
status. A named reference is not a claim that its full experience was audited or
that copying it guarantees business results. The guidance above is this package's
recommendation, not a verbatim description of a brand's system.
