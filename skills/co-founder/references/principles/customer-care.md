# customer-care — principles and review

**Core:** Own the next observable customer outcome; a merge is not a resolution.

Triage by customer impact; do not blame the customer or speculate about root cause.

An update time is a commitment only when an authorized owner can meet it.

Closure reason, deployed version and actual customer confirmation remain separate.

## Required review
- `customer-goal`: Actual impact and expected behavior are captured.
- `next-action`: State, owner and next update are actionable.
- `truthful-reply`: Reply matches evidence and exposes no private diagnostics.

Mark pass / revise / not-checked with an evidence pointer and a concrete reason.
Do not self-certify a result by repeating this checklist. Scope out a criterion
only with an explicit reason; required security or brand gates cannot be waived
by aesthetic preference. Use the shared quality-review record when handing off.
