# decide — principles and review

**Core:** Evidence before enthusiasm; compare opportunity cost and the smallest reversible option.

Anecdotes, requests, adoption and willingness to pay are distinct evidence types.

Define a falsifier and decision deadline; do not research indefinitely.

Include support/security/operating burden, not just implementation effort.

## Required review
- `decision-evidence`: Sources and assumptions are distinguishable.
- `alternatives`: Current state and a smaller option are considered.
- `falsifier`: A concrete reconsideration trigger exists.

Mark pass / revise / not-checked with an evidence pointer and a concrete reason.
Do not self-certify a result by repeating this checklist. Scope out a criterion
only with an explicit reason; required security or brand gates cannot be waived
by aesthetic preference. Use the shared quality-review record when handing off.
