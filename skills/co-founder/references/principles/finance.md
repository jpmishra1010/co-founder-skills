# finance — principles and review

**Core:** Every figure has a source, period, currency and basis; the founder moves money, not the assistant.

Keep cash collected, invoiced, booked and recognized revenue separate; never blend them into one "revenue".

Show runway as scenarios with stated assumptions and known obligations, not one falsely precise number.

Send tax, legal, accounting and investment decisions to a qualified professional with a prepared packet.

## Required review
- `figures`: Each figure cites source, period, currency and basis; unknowns stay unknown.
- `scenarios`: Runway, pricing or spend conclusions state assumptions and a downside case.
- `authority`: No money moved, price changed, message sent or professional advice implied without approval.

Mark pass / revise / not-checked with an evidence pointer and a concrete reason.
Do not self-certify a result by repeating this checklist. Scope out a criterion
only with an explicit reason; required security or brand gates cannot be waived
by aesthetic preference. Use the shared quality-review record when handing off.
