# founder-brief — principles and review

**Core:** Reduce decision load; rank by impact, urgency, dependencies and evidence freshness.

Use the project timezone and source timestamps; mark stale/inaccessible data.

Suggest fewer actions with reasons and no fabricated inbox, revenue or scheduling status.

Recommend no post when support, retention or a sales follow-up is a better use of time.

## Required review
- `freshness`: Source dates, timezone and gaps are explicit.
- `priorities`: A short ranked set has rationale and next actions.
- `boundaries`: No invented automation, account access or completed work.

Mark pass / revise / not-checked with an evidence pointer and a concrete reason.
Do not self-certify a result by repeating this checklist. Scope out a criterion
only with an explicit reason; required security or brand gates cannot be waived
by aesthetic preference. Use the shared quality-review record when handing off.
