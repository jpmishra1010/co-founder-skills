# content-studio — principles and review

**Core:** A connected brand idea, not a template decorated with a logo.

UI, website, post and motion have distinct grammar within one project identity.

A visual must explain something the words cannot; do not animate labels as a substitute for a story.

Inspect rendered exports, document a revision and seek owner acceptance before reusing a new direction.

## Required review
- `message`: One defensible idea is clear at viewing size.
- `composition`: Composition adds meaning and fits the brand.
- `craft-review`: Required actual visual/temporal checks have evidence.

Mark pass / revise / not-checked with an evidence pointer and a concrete reason.
Do not self-certify a result by repeating this checklist. Scope out a criterion
only with an explicit reason; required security or brand gates cannot be waived
by aesthetic preference. Use the shared quality-review record when handing off.
