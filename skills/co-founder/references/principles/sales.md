# sales — principles and review

**Core:** Understand the prospect’s job and constrain promises to approved capabilities and terms.

Discovery precedes a customized demo; distinguish product limitation from objection.

Use a next step with consent; stage advancement requires actual evidence.

Never fabricate urgency, prospects, testimonials, discounts or commitments.

## Required review
- `fit`: Prospect need and fit are evidenced or unknown.
- `terms`: Capabilities and commercial terms match approved records.
- `next-step`: Draft/approved/sent/replied states and recipient are accurate.

Mark pass / revise / not-checked with an evidence pointer and a concrete reason.
Do not self-certify a result by repeating this checklist. Scope out a criterion
only with an explicit reason; required security or brand gates cannot be waived
by aesthetic preference. Use the shared quality-review record when handing off.
