# Choose useful priorities from real current work

## Project binding and quality
Resolve the project using `references/project-identity.md`; bind this task to
its project ID, environment and, for visuals, the approved surface/brand bundle.
Read `references/principles/founder-brief.md`. Apply its evidence-backed review
before handoff. Wrong-project context or an unapproved consequential action
is a blocker, not a missing detail to guess.

## Use and context
Use for "today", a daily brief, a weekly review, or an explicitly scheduled
summary. Read company priorities and `.co-founder/state.json`, `commitments.json`,
`metrics.json`, relevant issues, release records, sales follow-ups, and experiment
results. Use connected data only with actual authorization. Missing data stays
unknown; do not invent activity. `cf.py brief` ranks local records including open
promises, and `cf.py metrics` reports latest values and staleness; neither
monitors external services.

## Procedure
1. Establish the current date/timezone and the period being reviewed. Check the
freshness of source records. Do not describe old notes as overnight developments.
2. Surface urgent customer/data/security impact first, then missed or due
commitments, blocked delivery, and valuable commercial opportunities. Report the
north-star metric and cash runway only from recorded snapshots, with their dates. Explain priority
using actual consequences and the founder's constraints rather than a made-up
numerical score. Identify contradictions with earlier decisions.
3. Select a small realistic set of next actions. Each needs the reason, owner,
source/evidence, and next observable result. Do not hand a solo founder a plan
requiring a full agency or five hours of daily content production.
4. Include at most one useful content opportunity when evidence supports it.
Separate a product announcement from a personal learning post. Do not produce
content for its own sake while a consequential customer problem is unresolved.
5. Identify decisions only the founder needs to make. State a recommendation
and alternatives; avoid repeatedly asking settled questions.
6. Weekly: compare plans to actual outcomes, unresolved/reopened incidents,
experiments, founder supervision time, and available usage records. Flag patterns
such as repeated fixes or campaigns based on unreleased features. Propose one
process improvement and prune obsolete context rather than adding more meetings.
7. Save a short dated handoff when authorized. Scheduling is separate setup,
requires a supported host scheduler, scoped permissions, timezone, and approval.
Do not claim the skill will run later just because a cadence is written here.

## Deliverable
A compact brief: immediate concern, top priorities with reasons, customer/sales
follow-up, one optional content opportunity, founder decision, and stale/missing
inputs. Use `founder-brief.md`. The brief should reduce coordination work.

## Done
The founder can act without rereading an entire chat history. An empty evidence
set yields a clear onboarding request, not invented customers, deadlines, or
revenue. Do not execute suggested sends, deployments, or publishing as part of
summarizing them unless separately authorized.
