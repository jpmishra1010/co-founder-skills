# Challenge an idea and research the smallest worthwhile move

## Project binding and quality
Resolve the project using `references/project-identity.md`; bind this task to
its project ID, environment and, for visuals, the approved surface/brand bundle.
Read `references/principles/decide.md`. Apply its evidence-backed review
before handoff. Wrong-project context or an unapproved consequential action
is a blocker, not a missing detail to guess.

## Use and context
Use for a proposed feature, product direction, pricing experiment, roadmap
choice, or request for independent judgment. Read the company brief, the stated
constraint, relevant customer evidence, and existing decisions. Do not inspect
all code or start building. Read `references/research-method.md` for external
research and `references/decision-quality.md` for major choices.

## Procedure
1. Restate the underlying customer job and the decision to make, not the solution
   the founder suggested. Identify the affected customer segment and what exists
   today. Recover repository/product answers before asking the founder.
2. Establish evidence: actual incidents, observed behavior, requests, conversion
   data, existing alternatives, and source dates. Distinguish complaints from
   buying intent; do not infer demand from popularity or a promotional post.
3. For external research, begin with a bounded set of current primary sources
   and relevant customer discussions. Inspect competing products' documented
   capabilities. Label blocked sources. Seek the strongest disconfirming case.
4. Compare at least the current state, a smaller solution, and the proposed
   solution for user value, adoption friction, cost, dependencies, security,
   support burden, reversibility, and lost opportunities. Use estimates as
   estimates. Do not invent precision or score a market without data.
5. Check the complete journey: who opts in, who grants permission, who recovers
   from an error, and what other modules must understand the change. Route to
   impact-map only if code or shared behavior matters to the decision.
6. Recommend build / simplify / experiment / postpone / reject. Explain the
   strongest counterargument to your own recommendation and what would change
   your view. Ask no more than three material questions at once; do not ask
   questions whose answers do not change the decision.
7. Propose a bounded experiment with a success measure, observation window,
   stop criterion, and owner. Never declare intent to implement as approval.

## Deliverable
A decision brief: problem, audience, evidence with sources/dates, assumptions,
options, recommendation, tradeoffs, risks, open questions, experiment, and
reconsideration trigger. Use `assets/templates/decision.md`. Save an approved
summary to the decision log, not the entire research transcript.

## Done / handoff
A founder can act on the recommendation and see its uncertainty. A feature plan
starts only after the idea/scope is authorized. Do not publish market-size claims,
guaranteed revenue, competitor superiority, or legal conclusions without evidence.

## Example
For automatic reordering, distinguish "avoid stockouts" from "authorize spending".
Investigate inventory accuracy and approval behavior before recommending
unattended purchase orders. A suggested-reorder queue may be the cheaper test;
that is a hypothesis to validate, not an automatic answer for every business.
