# Prepare truthful, relevant sales conversations and follow-ups

## Project binding and quality
Resolve the project using `references/project-identity.md`; bind this task to
its project ID, environment and, for visuals, the approved surface/brand bundle.
Read `references/principles/sales.md`. Apply its evidence-backed review
before handoff. Wrong-project context or an unapproved consequential action
is a blocker, not a missing detail to guess.

## Use and context
Use for a specific prospect, discovery call, tailored demo, proposal, objection,
or pipeline review. Read authorized prospect notes, approved offers/prices,
verified capabilities, and previous commitments. Treat absent CRM/email access
as absent. Do not fetch personal details unrelated to the sales task.

## Procedure
1. Establish the prospect's business job, workflow, pain, existing tools,
constraints, decision process, and next meeting/action. Separate observed facts
from assumptions. Do not manufacture urgency, buying intent, or authority.
2. Prepare discovery questions that reveal fit and consequences rather than lead
the prospect into agreement. Surface reasons the product may not fit.
3. Tailor the demonstration around one relevant end-to-end job using synthetic
or authorized demo data. Show actual available behavior, recovery, and limits.
Do not present prototypes/roadmap items as released features.
4. Match objections to evidence and tradeoffs. Compare competitors using current
primary sources; avoid unverified criticism. Route product gaps to decide,
not a promise that engineering will deliver them by a convenient date.
5. Draft the proposal with approved scope, assumptions, exclusions, deliverables,
commercial terms, responsibilities, support boundaries, and acceptance process.
Mark legal/tax terms requiring qualified review; do not improvise contracts.
Discounts, dates, refunds, and feature commitments need founder approval.
6. Prepare a specific follow-up with the agreed next step. Use the actual thread
and recipient through an authorized connector when available. Never send a
sequence or mass outreach without explicit audience/text approval and applicable
consent/platform checks. Respect opt-outs and approved communication policies.
7. Keep the pipeline concise: stage, evidence for stage, next action, date, owner,
blocker, and realistic unknowns. Share sanitized recurring objections with growth.

## Deliverable
A discovery brief, demo agenda, objection response, proposal draft, or follow-up
with evidence and approvals needed. Use `sales-brief.md`. No fabricated customer
logos, case studies, guarantees, lead lists, or sales forecasts.

## Done
The next conversation/action is clear and truthful. Distinguish drafted, approved,
sent, replied, and accepted. A prepared proposal is not a sale. An unresponsive
prospect is not an opportunity won or a reason for unlimited follow-ups.
